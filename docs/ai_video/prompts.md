# AI video promptlari — Blades of Anatolia: Ertugrul

Sinopsis treyleri, ~100 soniya, 16 ta kadr. Har kadr 5–8 soniya —
bu AI video vositalarining (Runway Gen-3, Kling, Luma, Veo, Sora) bir martalik
generatsiya oynasiga mos keladi. Uzunroq so'rasangiz sifat tushadi.

Promptlar **inglizcha** — hamma AI video modellari inglizchada aniqroq ishlaydi.
Har kadrga o'zbekcha izoh berilgan.

---

## Umumiy uslub bloki

Har promptning oxiriga qo'shing (o'zgartirmasdan) — kadrlar bir-biriga o'xshasin:

```
STYLE: cinematic historical drama, 13th-century Anatolia, anamorphic 2.39:1,
35mm film grain, warm golden-hour key light with cool shadow fill,
volumetric haze, shallow depth of field, muted earth palette —
felt cream, antique gold, turquoise accent, deep charcoal.
Handheld with subtle weight, no fast whip pans. Colour grade: warm highlights,
teal-lifted shadows. No text, no watermark, no modern objects.
```

**Negative prompt** (hamma kadrda bir xil):

```
modern clothing, wristwatch, plastic, cars, power lines, neon, anime,
cartoon, oversaturated, blurry, distorted hands, extra fingers, text overlay,
watermark, logo, low resolution, warped faces, duplicate limbs
```

## 1-BO'LIM — Dunyo (0:00–0:24)

### Kadr 01 — Ochilish: vodiy (7 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Quyosh botayotgan vodiy, tepalikdan pastga qarash. Oba ko'rinadi.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/02_map_wide_hills.png`, `reference/03_camp_crane_shot.png`

```
Slow aerial descent over a wide Anatolian valley at golden hour. Rolling green
hills covered in dark pine forest give way to a flat basin where a nomadic camp
of cream felt yurts is arranged in two concentric rings. Thin campfire smoke
drifts upward. Long shadows stretch east. The camera glides down and forward,
revealing the camp scale. No people yet.
```

### Kadr 02 — Oba ichida (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Oba ichi, kundalik hayot. Kamera odamlar orasidan sekin yuradi.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/01_map_camp_golden.png`

```
Steadicam push-in at eye level through a 13th-century Turkic nomad camp.
Cream felt yurts with carved wooden door frames on both sides. A woman carries
water, an old man mends a saddle, children run past. Smoke from a stone
campfire crosses the frame. Dust motes catch the low sun. Shallow focus on the
foreground yurt rope, camp soft behind.
```

### Kadr 03 — Qahramon (5 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Ertug'rul — orqadan, keyin yarim profil. Yuzi to'liq ko'rinmasin.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/04_parkour.png`

```
Low-angle tracking shot from behind a 13th-century Turkic warrior walking away
from camera through the camp. Dark red wool kaftan over a fur-trimmed shoulder
mantle, conical fur-lined cap, curved kilij sabre at the hip. He stops, turns
his head three-quarters into the light — face partly shadowed, not fully
revealed. Golden rim light on his shoulder.
```

### Kadr 04 — Tashvish (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Ufqda chang — kimdir kelmoqda. Tinchlik buziladi.

```
Wide static shot: the camp in the foreground, and on the far ridge line a thin
column of dust rises against the setting sun. A sentry on a wooden watch
platform turns sharply toward it. Wind moves the felt door flaps. The light
begins to cool. Slow, almost imperceptible push-in.
```

## 2-BO'LIM — Harakat (0:24–0:48)

### Kadr 05 — Yugurish (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Qahramon oba ichida yuguradi — kamera yonidan uchadi.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/04_parkour.png`

```
Fast lateral tracking shot alongside a running warrior weaving between felt
yurts and wooden carts. Camera matches his speed, yurts flick past in the
foreground creating natural wipes. Dust kicks from his boots. Golden hour
backlight flares between the tents. Motion has weight — he leans into the turn.
```

### Kadr 06 — Parkur (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** To'siqdan sakrab o'tish, devorga chiqish. Bir uzluksiz kadr.

```
Continuous handheld shot following a warrior vaulting a wooden fence, planting
one hand, landing in a roll, then running up a low stone wall and pulling
himself onto a flat roof. Camera stays with him, rising as he rises. Practical,
grounded movement with real weight — not superhero. Dust and loose straw
disturbed by each contact.
```

### Kadr 07 — Tepalikda (5 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Tepada to'xtaydi, pastdagi vodiyni ko'zdan kechiradi.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/05_eagle_vision.png`

```
The warrior crests a grassy ridge and stops, silhouetted against a burning
orange sky. Wind moves the tall grass around him and lifts his cloak. He scans
the valley below. Camera slowly orbits from behind him to a three-quarter
profile. Deep atmospheric haze separates the hill layers.
```

## 3-BO'LIM — Jang (0:48–1:24)

### Kadr 08 — Dushman (5 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Oq plashli Templar jangchilari. Sovuq, tahdidli.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/07_combat_templars.png`

```
Slow low-angle dolly past a line of armoured crusader knights in white surcoats
with dark red crosses, chainmail beneath, helms lowered. They advance through
drifting smoke at dusk. Cold blue-grey light on the mail contrasts the warm
firelight behind them. Their pace is unhurried and inevitable.
```

### Kadr 09 — Kamon (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Kamon tortish — nafas, titroq, otish.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/06_bow_aim.png`

```
Tight over-the-shoulder shot of a Turkic composite bow being drawn. Fingers
with a thumb ring lock the string, the bow limbs bend, the arrow shaft trembles
faintly with held breath. Focus racks from the arrowhead to a distant target.
Release: the string snaps forward, the arrow leaves frame, the archer's hand
opens. Golden dust in the air.
```

### Kadr 10 — Birinchi zarba (5 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Qilich to'qnashuvi. Uchqun. Og'irlik sezilsin.

```
Impact shot: a curved kilij meets a straight crusader sword edge-on. Sparks
burst at the contact point. The blades lock and grind. Both fighters' arms
shake with the strain. Shallow focus on the crossed steel, faces blurred
behind. Slight slow motion at the moment of contact, then back to full speed.
```

### Kadr 11 — To'liq jang (7 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Ko'p kishilik jang. Kamera qahramon bilan aylanadi.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/07_combat_templars.png`, `08_combat_night.png`

```
Single continuous orbiting shot around a Turkic warrior fighting three crusader
knights at once in a burning nomad camp at night. He parries, pivots, cuts low,
steps through. Firelight from an overturned brazier throws long moving shadows.
Sparks and embers drift. Camera circles him at chest height, always keeping him
centre frame. Grounded, heavy, exhausting combat — not acrobatic.
```

### Kadr 12 — Parry (5 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Mukammal parry — vaqt sekinlashadi.

```
Extreme slow motion: a descending crusader blade is deflected at the last
instant by an angled kilij. The impact sends a shower of sparks across the
frame and the attacker's weapon is thrown wide, opening his guard. Time returns
to normal speed as the counter begins. Firelight, embers, breath visible in
cold air.
```

### Kadr 13 — Yakunlovchi zarba (5 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Raqib muvozanatini yo'qotadi. Kamera pastdan.

```
Low-angle shot: an armoured knight staggers backward, off balance, weapon arm
dropping. The Turkic warrior steps into frame from the left, silhouetted
against fire, and raises his sabre. Cut on the movement before contact — do not
show the strike land. Embers rise. Sound of breath.
```

## 4-BO'LIM — Yakun (1:24–1:40)

### Kadr 14 — Bahosi (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Jangdan keyin. Yolg'iz, charchagan. G'alaba bepul emas.
> **Ma'lumotnoma:** `reference/09_death_screen.png`

```
Wide static shot at dawn: a lone warrior kneels in the ash of a burned camp,
sabre point in the ground, head lowered, shoulders moving with heavy breathing.
Thin smoke rises around him. Cold blue morning light replaces the fire.
Scattered debris, a torn banner. Absolute stillness. Slow push-in.
```

### Kadr 15 — Ko'chish (6 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Oba yo'lga chiqadi — hikoyaning davomi.

```
Extreme wide shot: a long caravan of nomads, horses, camels and loaded carts
moves along a ridge line at sunrise, silhouetted against a pale gold sky. The
column stretches from one side of frame to the other. Dust hangs behind them.
The camera holds still and lets them cross. Epic scale, quiet mood.
```

### Kadr 16 — Logo (4 s)
> **O'zbekcha:** Qora fon, logo paydo bo'ladi. Bu kadrni AI da EMAS,
> montajda `reference/11_logo_full.png` bilan qiling — logo aniq chiqishi kerak.

```
Black screen. The emblem fades in centred: a circular gold-ringed medallion
with two crossed curved sabres behind a central disc bearing the Kayi tribe
IYI tamga in antique gold. Below it the wordmark. Faint ember particles drift
across the black. Hold, then fade out.
```
